# Code of conduct

Word Crawler follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions; no harassment,
insults or personal attacks; no publishing of other people's private information.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/davide0707). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or
remove comments, close issues or block accounts that do not respect these rules.
